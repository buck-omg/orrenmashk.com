About OrrenMashk Group

A Veteran-led C-Corporation delivering doctrine, threat and red team work, operator training, disaster response coordination, and research and development.

The firm.

OrrenMashk Group, Inc. was founded by Veteran leaders whose operational backgrounds inform how the firm reads threats, builds doctrine, and trains operators. We work alongside government customers, critical infrastructure operators, and mission-aligned partners who require a disciplined, independent voice on uncrewed aircraft, counter-uncrewed aircraft, and critical infrastructure security.

Leadership.

Paul "Buck" Chambers, Chief Executive Officer

Retired United States Marine Corps Gunnery Sergeant. Purple Heart. Operational background in infantry, counterintelligence, and human intelligence. Buck's experience shapes how OrrenMashk analyzes threats, architects doctrine, and evaluates systems against real operational need rather than catalog positioning.

Daniel Peoples, Chief Operating Officer

United States Marine Corps and United States Army Veteran. Aviation background. Daniel's combined service and aviation experience add depth in flight operations, aerial capability development, and mission support, broadening OrrenMashk's operational reach.

Independence & discipline.

OrrenMashk Group does not resell platforms. We do not represent manufacturers. We do not take OEM margin on any system we assess or recommend. We maintain strict separation between our doctrine, threat, and training work and any adjacent fulfillment entity, so that our threat picture and our training content are not shaped by what someone else is selling.

Red team and operator-facing work runs under written scope, defined rules of engagement, and documented authorization. We decline work that cannot be properly scoped or that lacks the written authority to proceed.
