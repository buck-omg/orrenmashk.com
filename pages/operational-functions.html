Operational Functions

Four integrated operational functions, each a complete capability built around a specific mission need, deployable as configured or adapted to environment and requirement.

Immediate overhead awareness doctrine for dynamic incident scenes. Persistent ISR coverage planning deployed rapidly to support law enforcement, fire response, and emergency management operations from initial contact through incident resolution.
