Capability 01

Threat & Red Team

Counterintelligence and human intelligence-informed threat work for operators and owners of critical infrastructure, airborne reconnaissance programs, and counter-uncrewed aircraft postures.

OrrenMashk Group conducts threat and vulnerability work using an analytical, adversarial, doctrine-driven methodology. The output is a threat picture the customer can act on and a defense posture that holds up when it is tested.

Threat Assessment

We study adversary doctrine, intent, access, and capability against a specific target set. The counterintelligence and human intelligence lens shapes how we examine insider access, social engineering, open-source collection against the customer, and the human factors that drive how an adversary actually operates. The deliverable is a prioritized threat picture with named courses of action, not a generic risk register.

Red Team Planning and Execution

We plan and execute adversary emulation against uncrewed aircraft architectures, counter-uncrewed aircraft postures, and the operator and supervisor cells that run them. Execution is scoped and authorized in writing. We do not test against operational systems without the customer's written authorization, documented airspace coordination where applicable, and a signed scope agreement defining rules of engagement.

Scope Discipline Every red team engagement runs under a written statement of work, defined rules of engagement, and documented authorization. We decline work that cannot be properly scoped or that lacks the written authority to proceed.

Vulnerability Analysis for Critical Infrastructure

We assess perimeter, airspace, communications, and detect-identify-defeat postures for critical infrastructure sites and events. The analysis is architecture-level and vendor-agnostic. We tell you where the gaps are and what the adversary gets if the gap is not closed. The analysis supports both pre-incident posture improvement and post-incident after-action work.

Operator Threat Education

We build and deliver red team-informed curriculum for operators and supervisors. Content maps to threats we observe in assessment work, so the operator learns against a realistic adversary rather than a textbook one. Delivery sits on top of National Counter-UAS Training Center-aligned baseline content when the customer requires it, inside the customer's authorized training environment.

Why OrrenMashk

Veteran-led, with combat-deployed counterintelligence, human intelligence, and uncrewed systems operations background. Doctrine-driven and equipment-agnostic. No OEM dependency. No manufacturer positioning. Independent of the fulfillment side of the uncrewed systems ecosystem, so our threat picture is not shaped by what someone else is selling.

Engagement

Threat and Red Team work runs on a scoped statement of work with written authorization, defined rules of engagement, and documented reporting at close-out. Government program managers, critical infrastructure security leads, and operational commanders can reach us at the address below.
