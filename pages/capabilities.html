What We Do

Capabilities

Six integrated capability areas, each grounded in operational experience and delivered as doctrine, assessment, architecture, or operator-facing training.

01 / Threat & Red Team

Threat & Red Team

Counterintelligence and human intelligence-informed threat assessment. Red team planning and execution against uncrewed aircraft and counter-uncrewed aircraft postures. Vulnerability analysis for critical infrastructure. Operator threat education.

Full detail →
02 / Airborne Reconnaissance & ISR

Airborne Reconnaissance & ISR Doctrine

Persistent overhead coverage doctrine for tactical uncrewed aircraft, EO/IR sensor integration planning, AI-assisted target recognition workflow design, and GPS-denied operation planning for environments where standard platforms fail. Equipment-agnostic by design.

03 / Situational Awareness Systems

Situational Awareness Systems

Real-time common operating picture architecture combining resilient tactical mesh communications, mobile ad hoc network backbones, open-standard geospatial data layers, and operator-side displays sized for the mission. Doctrine first, equipment-agnostic by design.

04 / Integrated Perimeter Security

Integrated Perimeter Security

Combined aerial and ground-based surveillance architecture unifying uncrewed aircraft, compact radar for counter-uncrewed aircraft detection, EO/IR payloads, and resilient communications into a single persistent common operating picture for high-value sites.

05 / Rapid Disaster Response

Rapid Disaster Response Coordination

Faith-based humanitarian partner coordination doctrine. Prepositioned aerial intelligence framework. Geospatial data backbone coordination. Regional heavy-lift and airspace sponsor pathway planning. Red Cross coordination at the capability level.

06 / Operator Training Delivery

Operator Training Delivery

Operator and supervisor training layered on top of National Counter-UAS Training Center-aligned baseline content. Exercise design and facilitation. Red team-informed curriculum. Training delivery under 6 CFR 124.5 guidance; OrrenMashk delivers, does not certify.

Partner Citation Discipline OrrenMashk Group does not resell, represent, or carry OEM margin on any platform referenced in capability or doctrine work. Vendor and platform names appear in deliverables only where the customer has specified them or where an executed OrrenMashk-side agreement authorizes the reference.
