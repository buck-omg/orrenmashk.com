Who We Are

Mission-first. Always.

OrrenMashk Group was founded by Veteran leaders with complementary operational backgrounds and a shared commitment to disciplined, mission-focused work. Our lane is doctrine, threat and red team analysis, operator training delivery, disaster response coordination, and research and development.

We do not sell products. We do not resell platforms. We study the adversary, build the doctrine, train the operator, and architect the response, so that when the uncrewed aircraft lifts off or the sensor array activates, the people and the plan behind it are sound.

Research & Development

A doctrine-informed R&D vehicle.

OrrenMashk Group is a research and development prime vehicle for small business innovation research and commercial solutions opening topics in the uncrewed systems, counter-uncrewed systems, and critical infrastructure security spaces.

Our thesis is simple. The transition from non-kinetic to kinetic effector in counter-uncrewed aircraft work is a doctrine and operator-decision problem before it is an engineering problem. Simulation-layer research that treats operator decision architecture as the design surface, not an afterthought, is where meaningful progress on this transition will come from.

Government program managers with topics in doctrine-informed small innovation, simulation-layer effector transition, operator decision architecture, and critical infrastructure threat and red team methodology are invited to engage.
